OtvOS

Everything you love about tvOS. On the TV you have.

OtvOS replaces the home screen and adds the parts of Apple TV that make it a pleasure to use, built to stay quick even on a 1.4 GB streaming stick.

A home screen that feels right

A top shelf, tiles that lift under focus, and the focused app's colour across the room. Profiles follow your Google accounts.

Control Center

Hold Home. Power, Wi-Fi, Do Not Disturb, sleep timer, volume, AirPlay, Apple Home, and Close Background Apps when the TV needs breathing room.

Live TV from everywhere

Free channels from every country in an Apple TV-style app, with categories, search, favourites and channel up and down.

Your iPhone is the remote

Otv Remote pairs with a code, works from Control Center and the Lock Screen, types for you, and sends what you're watching to the TV.

OpenTV Companion

Your Mac and your TV, finally on speaking terms.

  • TV as DisplayExtend or mirror your Mac onto the TV, with the latency of a game stream. Over Wi-Fi or a USB-C cable.
  • The Mac's pointer on the TVPush the cursor past the top of your screen and keep going. Click, scroll and type on the TV.
  • Send to TVThe tab in your browser or the video in QuickTime or VLC, on the big screen in one click.
  • TV ManagerEvery app on the TV: open, stop, clear, turn off, uninstall, or drop an .apk to install. Tweaks, files and screenshots too.

Install

Set up in minutes.

The installer finds your TV, downloads the newest version of everything and sets it up. Run it again any time to update, or to put the TV back the way it was.

Turn on debugging

On the TV: Settings › System › About, press Android TV OS build seven times. Then in Developer options, turn on USB debugging and Wireless debugging.

Open the installer

Download OpenTV Installer, unzip it and open it. The first time, right-click it and choose Open.

Pick your TV

It finds the TV on your Wi-Fi, or over a USB-C cable. Choose what to install, press Install, and press Home on the remote.
