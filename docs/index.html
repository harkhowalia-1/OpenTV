OtvOS

A home screen worth coming home to.

OtvOS replaces your TV's home screen with one that's clean, quick and a joy to use, and it's tuned to fly even on a 1.4 GB streaming stick.

Your apps, front and centre

A top shelf for favourites, tiles that lift and glow under focus, and the focused app's colour washing across the room. Profiles follow the Google accounts on the TV.

Control Center

Hold Home. Power, sleep timer, Wi-Fi, Do Not Disturb, screensaver, volume, AirPlay, Apple Home, and Close Background Apps when the TV needs a breather. On a TV, Inputs sits right beside it: every HDMI port, AV and Live TV.

12,000 live channels

IPTV brings free channels from almost every country: categories, search, favourites and recently watched. Channel up and down while you watch.

Everything matches

Settings, an App Store, a clock screensaver and a Connect Remote screen, all in the same calm design.

Otv Remote

Your iPhone is the remote.

  • Swipe, tap, typeA touch surface, every button (Inputs too, on a TV with HDMI ports), and the iPhone's keyboard for search boxes.
  • Volume buttonsThe iPhone's own volume buttons change the TV's volume while the app is open.
  • Control Center and Lock ScreenRemote, play and pause, power and volume controls, plus Siri and Shortcuts.
  • Send to TVShare a YouTube video, a Netflix show, a song or a web page, and it opens on the TV.
  • Always readyPair once, reconnects by itself, wakes the TV. The whole family can connect at once.

OpenTV Companion

Your Mac and your TV, finally on speaking terms.

  • TV as DisplayExtend your Mac onto the TV as a second screen, mirror it, or put iPhone Mirroring on the big screen. Low latency over Wi-Fi, even lower over a USB-C cable.
  • Your pointer on the TVPush the cursor past the top of your screen and keep going. Push into the TV's left edge to go back, its top edge for Control Center; a two-finger click is Home.
  • Send to TVThe tab in any browser, or the video in QuickTime or VLC, carries on on the TV at the same moment.
  • TV ManagerEvery app on the TV: open, stop, clear, turn off, uninstall, or drop an .apk to install it. Resolution, animations, ad-blocking DNS, HDMI-CEC, screenshots and files.

Install

Pick your way in.

First, on the TV: Settings › System › About, press Android TV OS build seven times, then turn on USB debugging and Wireless debugging in Developer options. (Not needed for the no-computer way.)

Easiest · Mac

OpenTV Installer

  1. Download it, unzip, and open it (right-click › Open the first time).
  2. Pick your TV: it finds it on your Wi-Fi or over USB-C.
  3. Choose what to install and press Install. Run it again any time to restore the TV. Updates come to the TV by themselves: Settings › OtvOS Update.

No computer · TV

Straight from the TV

  1. Install Downloader from the TV's app store.
  2. Open harkhowalia-1.github.io/OpenTV/apk and install OtvOS. For IPTV, harkhowalia-1.github.io/OpenTV/iptv.
  3. Settings › System › Accessibility › OtvOS: turn it on. Press Home.
Any computer · adb

Command line

  1. Download OtvOS.apk, connect with adb connect <tv-address> and run:
adb install -r -g OtvOS.apk
adb shell settings put secure enabled_accessibility_services com.gathin.tvhome/com.gathin.tvhome.HomeKeyService
adb shell settings put secure accessibility_enabled 1
adb shell cmd package set-home-activity --user 0 com.gathin.tvhome/.MainActivity
adb shell appops set com.gathin.tvhome REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES allow
