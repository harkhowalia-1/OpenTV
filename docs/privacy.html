Privacy

Updated November 4, 2026. Applies to Otv Remote for iPhone and to the OpenTV apps for TV and Mac.

OpenTV doesn't collect anything. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The apps talk to your TV over your own network, and nothing is sent to us.

Otv Remote

  • Your TV, on your network. Otv Remote finds your TV on your Wi-Fi (this is why it asks for Local Network access) and sends it your button presses, text and links directly. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Pairing. When you pair, the TV gives your iPhone a key so only your devices can control it. The key stays on your iPhone and the TV.
  • Send to TV. A link you share to the TV goes only to your TV.
  • Signing in to Xbox Cloud Gaming. Microsoft's sign-in page opens inside the app, in a private browser that keeps nothing afterwards. The signed-in session is encrypted on your iPhone for your TV alone and sent straight to it over your network. Your password goes only to Microsoft.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. Actions you set up run on your iPhone and talk only to your TV.

On the TV

  • Wallpapers and screen savers are downloaded from Apple and Bing when you choose them. Cloud Gaming talks to Microsoft's Xbox services with your own sign-in, to show your games, friends and achievements. IPTV plays channels directly from the broadcasters that publish them. Those services handle requests under their own privacy policies.
  • Software updates are downloaded from OpenTV's releases on GitHub.
  • OpenTV Companion for Mac talks to the TV over your network or a USB-C cable.

Children

OpenTV collects no information from anyone, including children.

Questions
